1. ### Summary of Relevant Literature on Deep Learning in NSCLC Image Analysis
2. 1. **Title**: Deep segmentation networks predict survival of non-small cell lung cancer
3.    - **Authors**: Stephen Baek et al.
4.    - **Published**: 2019-11-08
5.    - **Summary**: This study demonstrates that convolutional neural networks (CNNs), specifically a U-Net model, can effectively segment tumors in PET/CT images of NSCLC patients. The CNN identified survival-related image features that correlated strongly with overall and disease-specific survival rates. The findings suggest that CNNs can enhance prognostic capabilities in NSCLC without relying on additional clinical information.
6.    - **Link**: [Read the full paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.03456)
7. 2. **Title**: Multimodal Learning for Non-small Cell Lung Cancer Prognosis
8.    - **Authors**: Yujiao Wu et al.
9.    - **Published**: 2022-11-07
10.    - **Summary**: This paper introduces a cross-modality network, Lite-ProSENet, for survival time analysis in lung cancer, integrating both textual clinical data and visual scans. The model outperformed existing methods, achieving a new state of the art in survival analysis for NSCLC patients, indicating the potential of multimodal deep learning approaches in improving diagnostic accuracy.
11.    - **Link**: [Read the full paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.03456)
12. 3. **Title**: DeepMMSA: A Novel Multimodal Deep Learning Method for Non-small Cell Lung Cancer Survival Analysis
13.    - **Authors**: Yujiao Wu et al.
14.    - **Published**: 2021-06-12
15.    - **Summary**: This research presents a novel multimodal deep learning method for analyzing survival in NSCLC patients. The method leverages both imaging and clinical data to improve prognostic accuracy, addressing the challenges of delayed diagnosis and poor prognosis in lung cancer.
16.    - **Link**: [Read the full paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.05956)
