1. 1. **Deep Segmentation Networks Predict Survival of Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer** (2019) - Stephen Baek et al.: This study shows that CNNs, specifically the U-Net algorithm, can segment tumors in PET/CT images and identify survival-related features. The CNN was trained on tumor contours provided by physicians, correlating strongly with 2- and 5-year survival rates in a study of 96 NSCLC patients. The findings suggest enhanced prognostic capabilities beyond traditional features.
2. 2. **Customizing Knowledge Graph Embedding to Improve Clinical Study Recommendation** (2022) - Xiong Liu et al.: This research introduces custom2vec, a framework that customizes knowledge graph embeddings to improve clinical trial recommendations for NSCLC. The study demonstrates that incorporating user preferences into the training of embeddings leads to better performance in recommending trials, particularly for immuno-oncology therapies.
3. 3. **Comprehensive Joint Modeling of First-Line Therapeutics in Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer** (2024) - Benjamin Schneider et al.: This article discusses the high failure rates in first-line therapies for NSCLC due to resistance. The authors propose a joint modeling approach to better understand therapeutic outcomes and improve predictions in clinical settings.
